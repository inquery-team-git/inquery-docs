## Available Services

<Info>
  Services can be enabled per case (or set to always-on by your organization). If a service is configured as <b>ALWAYS</b>, you may not see it as a checkbox.
</Info>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Indexing (Default)" icon="list-check">
    **What it does**

    - Creates a basic index of all documents in the case
    - Identifies document types (medical records, bills, correspondence, etc.), dates, document authors, and authoring institutions
    - Organizes documents chronologically
    - Provides document-level summaries

    **When to use**

    - Always included by default
    - Essential for document organization
    - Required for efficient case review

    **Output**

    - Organized document index
    - Document type classifications
    - Basic document metadata

    <Info>
      Indexing is typically configured to be always included for your case type.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Medical Chronology" icon="timeline">
    **What it does**

    - Extracts medical events from your documents and builds a chronological timeline of care
    - Identifies doctor visits, diagnoses, treatments/procedures, medications, tests/imaging, and hospitalizations

    **When to use**

    - Personal injury cases
    - Medical malpractice cases
    - Workers' compensation cases
    - Any matter where understanding the medical timeline is critical

    **Output**

    - Detailed medical timeline
    - Event-level summaries
    - Provider and facility information
    - Diagnosis and treatment tracking

    **Dependencies**

    - Required if you select **Demand Letters**
    - Automatically checked when **Demand Letters** is selected

    ![Medical Chronology Example](/images/medical-chronology-sample.png)

    <Warning>
      If you uncheck Medical Chronology, Demand Letters will also be unchecked automatically since demand letters require medical chronology data.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Case Info (Structured Extraction)" icon="table">
    **What it does**

    - Extracts structured case facts from your documents, including:
      - Patient demographics (name, DOB, address)
      - Accident/incident details
      - Insurance information
      - Provider information
      - Key dates and locations
      - Injury descriptions

    **When to use**

    - Quick access to key case facts
    - Case summary preparation
    - Populating case management systems
    - Reporting and analytics

    **Output**

    - Structured data fields
    - Key dates and parties
    - Insurance details
    - Extracted case facts

    ![Case Info Extraction](/images/case-info-structured.png)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Demand Letters" icon="file-pen">
    **What it is**

    - AI-generated demand letters based on the medical record
    - Customized by claim type
    - Includes medical summary, damages, and settlement demand

    <Info>
      Demand Letters are available only if enabled for your organization (<code>show_demand_letter</code>).
    </Info>

    **Requirements**

    - Demand Letters must be enabled for your organization
    - **Medical Chronology** must be selected (required dependency)

    ### Demand Letter Types

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Bodily Injury (BI)" icon="scale-balanced">
        <b>Use for:</b> Standard bodily injury claims against an at-fault party<br/>
        <b>Best for:</b> Auto accidents, slip and falls, general negligence
      </Card>
      <Card title="Uninsured Motorist (UM)" icon="car-burst">
        <b>Use for:</b> Claims when the at-fault driver has no insurance<br/>
        <b>Best for:</b> No coverage or hit-and-run scenarios
      </Card>
      <Card title="Underinsured Motorist (UIM)" icon="shield">
        <b>Use for:</b> Claims when at-fault limits are insufficient<br/>
        <b>Best for:</b> Serious injuries exceeding policy limits
      </Card>
      <Card title="PIP/No-Fault (PIP/NF)" icon="file-shield">
        <b>Use for:</b> Personal Injury Protection / No-Fault claims<br/>
        <b>Best for:</b> No-fault jurisdictions and benefit maximization
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="What each letter includes">
        - Liability or coverage framing (depending on type)
        - Medical treatment overview (from Medical Chronology)
        - Special damages (e.g., medical bills, lost wages)
        - General damages (e.g., pain and suffering)
        - Settlement demand with justification
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Selecting multiple demand letter types">
        You can select multiple demand letter types for a single case. This is useful when:

        - You’re pursuing multiple claims (e.g., third-party liability + UIM)
        - You want multiple demand strategies
        - Your case may involve multiple insurance policies
      </Accordion>

      <Accordion title="Output">
        - Complete demand letter for each type selected
        - Medical summary section
        - Damages calculation
        - Professional formatting ready for review
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>

    ![Demand Letter Selection](/images/demand-letter-multiselect.png)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Service Selection Best Practices

<Tip>
  <b>Start with the basics</b>

  - Always include <b>Indexing</b> (usually automatic)
  - Add <b>Medical Chronology</b> for medical-driven matters
  - Add <b>Case Info</b> for quick reference and reporting
  - Add <b>Demand Letters</b> when you're ready to negotiate settlement

  <br/><br/>
  <b>You can always add more services later</b> using the “Request Additional Services” feature.
</Tip>

## Uploading Additional Files

After creating a case, you can upload additional documents at any time.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When to Upload Additional Files" icon="upload">
    **Common scenarios**

    - New medical records received from providers
    - Updated billing statements
    - Correspondence or legal documents
    - Expert reports
    - Supplemental medical evaluation
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
