## Opening a Document Section

Document sections in the left panel can be expanded to view their summaries and metadata.

### Expanding a Section

Click on any document section to expand it and view:

- AI-generated summary
- Document metadata (date, provider, institution, pages)
- Section tabs (Summary, Findings, Billing Info)
- Action buttons (Edit, Feedback, Citations)

![Expanded Document Section](/images/expanded-section.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Section header components">
    **Collapsed view shows:**

    - **Document Type Badge** – Color-coded category (Progress Note, Lab Result, etc.)
    - **Title** – Document description or auto-generated title
    - **Date** – Document date (if available)
    - **Page Range** – Start and end page numbers
    - **Relevance Badge** – High or Low relevance indicator
    - **Provider/Institution** – If enabled for your organization
    - **Expand Arrow** – Click anywhere on the header to toggle

    ![Section Header](/images/section-header.png)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Collapsed vs expanded states">
    **Collapsed (default):**

    - Single-line header only
    - Optimized for scanning large document lists

    **Expanded:**

    - Full summary displayed
    - Tabs and action buttons visible
    - Multiple sections can be expanded at once

    <Tip>
      **Performance tip:** Expanding many sections at once can slow scrolling. Collapse sections you are not actively reviewing.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Section tabs">
    Expanded sections include tabs for different content views.

    **Summary tab (default):**

    - AI-generated narrative summary
    - Structured with headers and bullet points

    **Findings tab (if available):**

    - Structured medical findings
    - Diagnosis codes such as ICD-10

    **Billing Info tab (billing documents only):**

    - Charges and payment information
    - CPT and billing-related data

    ![Section Tabs](/images/section-tabs.png)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Summary Display

Summaries appear in the expanded section with rich formatting.

**Formatting elements include:**

- **Headers** for logical sections
- **Bold text** for important findings
- **Bullet points** for lists
- **Paragraphs** for narrative detail

**Example summary structure:**

Chief Complaint\
• Patient presents with chest pain

Examination\
Patient reports substernal chest pressure radiating to left arm.\
Vital signs: BP 140/90, HR 88, RR 16.

Assessment\
Possible acute coronary syndrome. ECG shows ST elevation in leads II, III, aVF.

Plan\
• Admit to CCU\
• Start heparin drip\
• Cardiology consult

## Summary Validation Status

<Warning>
  **Invalid summary warning (Admin or Validator only)**

  You may see a message indicating that pages were added or removed. This means the summary may no longer reflect the full section content and should be regenerated.
</Warning>

**What to do:**

- Regenerate the executive summary to update all invalid section summaries
- Or proceed if changes were minor and accuracy is acceptable

## Medical Chronology Mode Summaries

Medical Chronology Mode provides a timeline-oriented view with structured summary columns.

### What this mode does

- Shows only medical document types
- Automatically sorts by date (oldest to newest)
- Displays summaries in a table format
- Excludes non-medical documents such as billing and correspondence

### Entering and exiting

- Click the **Clock icon** in the toolbar to enter
- Click the icon again or press **Escape** to exit

![Medical Chronology Mode](/images/med-chron-mode.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Customizing summary columns">
    **Default columns:**

    - Summary
    - Date

    **Custom columns (Admin or Validator):**

    - Configure columns such as Medications, Diagnoses, or Vital Signs
    - Save configuration to regenerate summaries in table format

    ![Custom Columns Modal](/images/medchron-columns-modal.png)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Best practices">

## Editing a Summary

You can manually edit any section summary.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the section">
    Expand the document section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit">
    Double-click the summary text to enter edit mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or cancel">
    Save with **Ctrl + Enter** or **Cmd + Enter**, or click away. Press **Escape** to cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Editing Summary](/images/edit-summary.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can edit summaries">
    **All authenticated users** can edit summaries.

    <Note>
      Changes save immediately and are visible to all users with access.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Markdown support">
    Summaries support markdown formatting such as headers, bold text, and bullet points.

    ```
    ## Examination
    Patient reports **severe headache**.
    • BP: 140/90
    • HR: 88
    ```
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Edit vs regenerate">
    **Edit manually** for small corrections or added context.\
    **Regenerate** when summaries are invalid or significantly incorrect.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Summary Citations (Explain Source)

<Note>
  **Admin-only feature.** Summary citations trace claims back to source pages in the PDF.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select summary text">
    Highlight at least ten characters in the summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Explain Source">
    Button appears when text is selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results">
    Quotes, page numbers, and reasoning appear below the summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Citation Results](/images/citation-results.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Using citation results">
    - Quotes are clickable and jump to the source page
    - Multiple supporting quotes may be returned
    - No results may indicate a generalized or unsupported claim
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When citations help">
    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Accuracy checks" icon="check-circle">
        Validate AI summaries before use in reports
      </Card>
      <Card title="Source discovery" icon="magnifying-glass">
        Find original context quickly
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Providing Feedback

You can submit feedback directly from a section summary.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand section">
    Open the document section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional selection">
    Highlight any problematic text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit feedback">
    Click the feedback button and complete the form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Feedback Modal](/images/feedback-modal.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Feedback best practices">
    Be specific, highlight the issue, and explain expected versus actual behavior.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens next">
    Feedback is sent to the InQuery team for triage and follow-up.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Executive Summary

The Executive Summary provides a case-level synthesis of all section summaries.

![Executive Summary Location](/images/executive-summary-location.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Executive vs section summaries">
    Executive summaries cover the entire case, while section summaries focus on individual documents.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Regenerating the executive summary">
    Regenerate after new uploads, service changes, or when summaries are invalid.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Custom instructions">
    Add instructions to focus the summary on injuries, causation, or specific findings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Group Summaries

Group summaries synthesize multiple sections, such as hospital admissions or date ranges.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create groups">
    Use automatic or manual grouping in Medical Chronology Mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process groups">
    Click the process button to generate summaries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results">
    Group summaries appear in group headers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Issues and Solutions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Summary seems inaccurate">
    Regenerate, edit manually, or submit feedback if issues persist.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Explain Source returns no results">
    Try selecting different text or manually review the document.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cannot edit summary">
    Ensure you are double-clicking and using a supported browser.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
