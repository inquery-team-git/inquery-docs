InQuery is an AI-powered medical records management platform designed for workers’ compensation professionals, medical-legal reviewers, and healthcare legal teams. The platform helps you rapidly process, analyze, and generate insights from large volumes of medical records for Independent Medical Evaluations (IMEs) and claims cases.

## What InQuery Does

InQuery transforms hours or days of manual medical record review into minutes by:

- **Organizing Records Automatically**  
  Upload medical records (PDFs) and let AI automatically classify documents by type (clinical notes, imaging reports, lab results, etc.), extract key dates, and organize everything into a structured format.

- **Generating Summaries**  
  Get AI-powered executive summaries of entire cases, individual document summaries, and medical chronologies that synthesize thousands of pages into actionable insights.

- **Finding Information Fast**  
  Search across all documents in a case instantly, with every AI-generated fact linked back to its source page for verification.

- **Creating Professional Reports**  
  Generate medical chronologies, executive summaries, demand letters, and custom reports with a few clicks. Download in multiple formats (PDF/DOCX) ready for stakeholders.

- **Enabling Team Collaboration**  
  Share cases with colleagues, track case progress, manage deadlines, and maintain audit trails across your organization.

## Who Uses InQuery

- **Claims Adjusters** – Review medical records quickly to make informed claim decisions  
- **IME Physicians** – Prepare for evaluations with organized case summaries  
- **Attorneys & Paralegals** – Build case strategies with medical chronologies and comprehensive record analysis  
- **Utilization Review Specialists** – Evaluate treatment necessity with rapid access to relevant clinical information  

## How It Works

1. **Create a Case** – Set up an IME with patient information, case type, and key dates  
2. **Upload Records** – Add medical records (supports multi-file PDFs of any size)  
3. **AI Processing** – InQuery automatically extracts text, classifies documents, generates summaries, and identifies key findings  
4. **Review & Analyze** – Navigate organized records, search for specific information, and validate AI-generated insights  
5. **Generate Reports** – Create professional deliverables including chronologies, executive summaries, and custom reports  
6. **Collaborate & Complete** – Share with team members, track progress, and export final documentation  

## Key Benefits

- **Save Time** – Reduce medical record review time by 60–80%  
- **Ensure Accuracy** – Every AI-generated fact links to its source page for easy verification  
- **Stay Compliant** – HIPAA compliant and SOC 2 certified platform  
- **Work Anywhere** – Cloud-based platform accessible from any device  
- **Scale Efficiently** – Handle cases with thousands of pages without performance issues
